Home

Home is a private household application. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Home reads the headers (sender, subject, date) and a short snippet of unread mail in one household Gmail account, using read-only access. This data is stored only on the household's own server, is never shared with anyone, and is used only to show the household its unread mail. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
